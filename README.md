# 🌿 Bonsai Therapy VR

### *Find your inner peace, one cut at a time.*

Step into a serene, digital dojo where time slows down. **Bonsai Master VR** isn't just a simulator; it’s a tactile meditation. Forget high scores and timers—focus on the grain of the wood, the shape of the canopy, and the perfect silhouette of your miniature tree.

---

## ✨ The peaceful VR Experience

This isn't a game you play with a mouse. It’s a craft you master with your hands.

* **Tactile Precision:** Use professional pruning shears with 1:1 motion tracking. Feel the resistance and the satisfying "click" through haptic feedback as you refine your masterpiece.
* **True 3D Artistry:** Peer through the branches, lean in to spot hidden leaves, and move around your workspace. In VR, depth perception is your most important tool.
* **Minimalist Beauty:** Immerse yourself in a clean, distraction-free environment designed to lower your heart rate and sharpen your focus.
* **Instant Gratification:** Watch your bonsai transform in real-time. Every cut is permanent, making every decision meaningful.

---

## 🛠 Handcrafted Mechanics

* **Natural Interactions:** Pick up, rotate, and swap tools effortlessly using the intuitive **XR Interaction system**.
* **Precision Cutting:** A custom-built collision system ensures that you only trim exactly what you touch.
* **Fluid Performance:** Optimized for high-framerate VR to ensure your creative flow is never interrupted by lag.

---

## 🎮 How to Begin

1. **Enter the Dojo:** Put on your headset and find your center.
2. **Equip Your Tools:** Reach out and grab your shears from the table.
3. **Sculpt:** Study the tree. Align your blades. Pull the trigger to make your mark.
4. **Admire:** Step back and view your creation from any angle.
